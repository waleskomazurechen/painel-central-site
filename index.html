One dashboard for every channel

Painel Central is a desktop dashboard that follows each video from production to publication, posts it to the connected networks and brings the results back to one screen.

Who uses it: Painel Central runs on its operator's own computer and manages the operator's own channels. It has no public sign-up and is not sold as a service.

Features

  • Production See every video, from idea to final file, reading the channel folders on the computer.
  • Publishing Approve a video or set a channel to automatic mode, and it goes out on that channel's schedule.
  • Metrics Followers, views, likes, comments and shares per channel and per video.
  • Alerts A clear warning when a connection expires or a post fails, and what to do about it.

How it works

Painel Central is installed on one Windows computer and opens in the browser at a local address. There is no shared server: data stays on that computer, and the app talks directly to each network's official API.

TikTok connection

  • Sign-in: the account owner signs in through TikTok's own authorization page and chooses to allow access. Painel Central never sees the TikTok password.
  • Account details: the app reads the account name and follower count to show which account is connected and how it is growing.
  • Posting: scheduled videos go to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts, and the account owner finishes and posts them in TikTok. For a direct post, the owner opens the posting window, reviews the video, edits the caption, chooses the privacy and interaction settings and confirms each post. The app adds no watermark to videos.
  • Results: for videos posted through the app, it reads the link and the view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Leaving: the connection can be removed in the app at any time, and access can also be revoked in the TikTok app.
