Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what Painel Central ("the app") collects, how it is used and how to have it removed. The app runs on its operator's own computer. It has no shared server and no public accounts.

What the app collects from TikTok

  • Account: open ID, display name, profile photo, follower count and public video count.
  • Posting settings: creator name, username, profile photo, and the privacy and interaction options TikTok allows for the account. These are read when the posting window opens and are not stored.
  • Videos posted through the app: video ID, share link, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Recent public videos: title, cover image, share link, creation date, and view, like, comment and share counts. These are shown in the posting window and are not stored.
  • Access tokens: the tokens TikTok issues after you authorize the app.

The app does not collect your TikTok password, your messages, your contacts, or the identity of people who watch or comment.

How it is used

  • To show which account is connected.
  • To publish the videos approved or scheduled in the app.
  • To show how each channel and video is performing.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to build profiles of viewers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a database on the operator's computer. Tokens and app secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. A daily copy of that database is kept in the operator's private cloud folder, with tokens still encrypted.

Who receives it

  • TikTok: receives the video, caption and requests needed to publish and read results.
  • Anthropic (Claude): the app's AI assistant may process metrics and video details when the operator asks it a question or gives it a task. Tokens are not shared with it.

Nothing else is shared, unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Removing the connection deletes the tokens from the app. Metrics already collected stay until deletion is requested, and backup copies are replaced within 14 days.

Your choices

  • Remove the connection in the app, or revoke access in your TikTok settings, at any time.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing the address below.
  • If you are in Brazil, you have the rights set out in the LGPD (Law 13.709/2018).

Children

The app is not meant for children, and it does not knowingly collect their data.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Changes and contact

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Questions or requests: hellolittleheart.contact@gmail.com
